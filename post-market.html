B. 30-Second Conclusion

The Anthropic trade ran for a second day: memory, semis and Korea surged while the funders of AI spending (Meta) and the Dow's Boeing drag kept the headline indexes from running away. Breadth stayed narrow — this is a selective, large-cap-led rally, not a broad melt-up. The real test is Wednesday: the FOMC decision at 2:00 p.m. ET, Chair Warsh's press conference at 2:30, and Microsoft and Alphabet earnings after the close.
