C. Macro Day Review

High impact Fed repricing: October hold / December hike is the consensus

A Reuters poll (Oct 16–20, 100 economists): 94 expect the Oct 27–28 FOMC to hold at 3.75–4.00%, and all 100 expect a +25bp hike in December (fewer than half expected that in September). ~60% see the terminal rate at 4.00–4.25%. Backdrop: September's first hike since 2023, September CPI 3.7% / PPI 6.3% both hot, and PCE expected to run 100bp+ above target for several quarters. The Fed is in blackout Oct 17–29, so pricing is poll-driven: ~84–88% odds of an October hold on prediction markets, ~76% for a December 25bp hike. (Wealth Professional, Prediction Markets Picks)

Medium impact Bond market stress: 20Y auction, France 10Y > 5%

The Oct 21 20-year auction was mixed: solid 2.73 bid-to-cover but the highest auction yield since 1986. Reuters' Trading Day noted the French 10Y breached 5% for the first time since 2002 with the widest German–French spread of this cycle — fiscal risk is live ahead of Moody's France review on Friday Oct 23. U.S. 10Y sat at ~5.15%, ~3bp higher on the day. (Reuters, Morningstar/Dow Jones)

High impact Iran / Israel: oil at five-month highs

Escalation cycle continued: Iran's defense minister said the country is "preparing for a fourth round of war"; Houthis hit Saudi Aramco's Yanbu and Shaybah facilities; Iran's state media claimed two tankers were hit in the Strait of Hormuz; the IRGC warned about seven subsea cables under Hormuz. Trump's pledge of no new Iran strikes before Nov 3 set a timeline. Brent intraday $111.95, closing ~$109–110; WTI ~$97–101 — five-month highs. (Morningstar/Dow Jones)

Notable China: LPR unchanged; domestic-demand plan

China's 1Y/5Y LPR held for the 17th straight month (3.0% / 3.5%); the PBoC is advancing its "re-anchoring" with the overnight repo as the main policy rate; the NDRC is drafting a 2026–2030 domestic-demand strategy with 136 initial application scenarios; 18 actively-managed ETFs were approved for Oct 23 launch.
