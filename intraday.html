All parameters below follow the standing Short-Option Strategy Playbook (spreads only, never naked; max loss 1–2% per trade; DTE 14–30 days; take profit at 50–70% of credit). Regime read: a vol-expansion day, not a day to add size into the fade

VIX ~19.8 intraday (+~15%) after a 17.20 close is a real vol expansion, and the front of the VIX curve has inverted (Nov 19.21 > Dec 19.01) while the longer curve stays in contango. Premiums on the 3x books are richer than yesterday — but they are richer because the underlying is falling 7–23% a day, and the weekend gap risk (midterms Tuesday, possible Iran headlines, Apple Monday) is exactly the risk that a short spread cannot outrun. Analysis judgement: the edge today is in not chasing the move. Any new spread sold into this tape is selling into a trend that has already run two days, with a three-day weekend gap in front of it. The playbook's rule — "sell the rip, not the crash" — is the operative one today.

KORU −15.0% to $10.82 on KOSPI −5.25%, and it is −48.7% in a month with a 30-day annualised vol of 134.2% and a 192.7% annualised vol.
