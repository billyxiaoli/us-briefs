GOOG is down 11.5% intraday after a Q3 miss (revenue $127.6B, GAAP EPS $2.12), a second straight quarter of negative free cash flow (−$9.07B), and a 2027 capex guide of "significantly higher." Trade the session as positioning into those prints, not as a verdict on them.

C. Macro News Intraday (Detailed)

1. The Fed held — with two hawkish dissents, and the market is pricing a December hike, not a cut

The FOMC held the target range at 3.75–4.00% on Wednesday, by a 10–2 vote. Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissented in favour of an immediate 25bp hike. The statement upgraded activity to "strong" (from "solid"); Chair Kevin Warsh said inflation is "too high" and gave no December commitment. From next fall, one FOMC meeting per year will carry no rate vote. The next meeting is December 8–9, and December-hike odds are now priced near 83–85%. The framing for the rest of the week is hike risk, not cut expectations. Source: Lesprom / Fed coverage · Source: Barron's live coverage

2. The 08:30 ET data dump: strong growth, sticky inflation, soft income — a combination that helps no one cleanly

Q3 GDP (advance) grew +3.4% annualised, beating the 3.1–3.2% consensus (Q2 +2.2%), driven by consumer spending +3.8% and private investment +19.4% (equipment +20.8–20.9%, the AI buildout), partly offset by a −3.32pp net-export drag. September PCE: headline +0.5% month over month, +3.6% year over year (August 3.4%); core +0.3% month over month, +3.1% year over year (August 3.0%) — both essentially in line. Energy is the wedge: gasoline +9.2% month over month, +35.4% year over year. Personal income rose only +0.2% (miss vs 0.3%), and the saving rate fell to 3.8%, the lowest since October 2022. Initial claims for the week ended October 24 were 194,000 (vs ~199–200k expected). The read: growth is not the problem, inflation is not cooling fast, and the consumer's buffer is thinning — which is why the 10Y is pinned near 5% rather than rallying on the GDP beat. Source: Investor's Business Daily · Source: BlazeTrends · Source: MBA commentary

3. ECB held at 2.50% — and Lagarde announced she will leave in February 2027

The ECB held its deposit rate at 2.50% (refi 2.65%, marginal lending 2.90%) and warned energy prices pose upside inflation risk (eurozone September CPI 3.8%, the highest in three years). The bigger story is succession: President Christine Lagarde resigned and will leave on February 7, 2027, ahead of France's presidential election. The euro fell to its lowest since May, ~$1.11–1.113. December ECB hike pricing is disputed across sources (~65% to ~84%) — do not quote a single number for it. Source: Reuters · Source: Economies.com
