F. Short-Option Strategy — Read-Only Analysis (Not a Trade Order)

Read-only, no trading action taken. This chapter is strategy analysis only, following the standing playbook (spreads only, never naked short puts/calls; single-trade max loss 1–2% of account; DTE 14–30 days; exit at 50–70% of credit). Direction follows market state, not a fixed annual bias. For anything only the broker app can confirm — positions, expiry, assignment, margin — check the IBKR app; nothing here asserts it.

Regime read: vol is paid for Wednesday, and Tuesday's bounce is not the time to lean in

VIX is reported at conflicting vintages (17.87 Tuesday vs 19.45 Friday close), so the honest regime read is "elevated, direction uncertain" rather than a single number — but the event wall is not uncertain at all: FOMC Wednesday 14:00 ET, MSFT/GOOG after the close, SK Hynix Wednesday, SNDK Thursday, GDP/PCE Thursday. Selling premium into that wall on Tuesday morning means getting paid Monday's vol for Wednesday's gap. The playbook discipline for this week, unchanged from Monday: if you sell premium at all, sell it after the event wall where possible, or size it so a Wednesday gap does not matter. Selling the rip into Wednesday's close, rather than chasing Tuesday's memory-led bounce, is the cleaner expression of the same view.

KOSPI +3.1% Tuesday after −1.46% Monday is a two-day round trip of ~4.5 points in the underlying proxy — exactly the overnight-gap regime the playbook prices (annualized vol 192.7%, 30-day vol 134.2%, 63% of days move >±5%, historic worst day −41.9%).
