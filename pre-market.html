The defining macro-market story is a repricing of who pays for the AI buildout. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang told CNBC on Oct 22 that AI demand is "off the charts" but capital availability for AI infrastructure is "becoming a challenge" — specifically that neoclouds lack working capital to fund construction. That comment landed alongside a string of credit-concentration headlines: Nvidia's 10-Q disclosed up to $164.5B in contingent AI-data-center guarantees (including ~$105B backstopping OpenAI's Ohio campus lease), CoreWeave's 2031/32 notes trading below 81¢, and Microsoft terminating its Nscale Loughton (Essex) agreement over power-delivery delays into the 2030s.
